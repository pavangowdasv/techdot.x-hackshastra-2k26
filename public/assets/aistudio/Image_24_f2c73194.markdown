## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-density, mission-critical command aesthetic for municipal intelligence and automated civil remediation. Balancing cold, dark-mode civic operational infrastructure with precise neon telemetry, the visual language merges **Futuristic Enterprise Glassmorphism** with **Technical Precision Minimalism**.

### Target Audience & Core Persona
1. **Citizens:** Require transparent, immediate feedback on neighborhood issues with accessible status tracking and trust-building visual verification.
2. **Operations Managers & Dispatchers:** Command-center operators requiring telemetry density, multi-layered geo-spatial radar maps, AI triage recommendations, and real-time SLA risk monitoring.
3. **Field Technicians & Department Workers:** On-site crews needing rapid, high-contrast, glanceable work orders with direct tactile state updates and visual documentation tools.

### Emotional Signature
- **Vigilant & Unfaltering:** Evokes the operational authority and readiness of an urban telemetry hub.
- **Intelligent & Deterministic:** AI interventions appear systematic, quantifiable, and transparent rather than speculative.
- **Sleek & Hyper-Refined:** Deep slate voids illuminated by fine luminous borders, controlled neon halos, and crisp data typography.

## Colors

The palette operates under a default dark regime engineered to reduce ocular fatigue in 24/7 control room environments while sustaining contrast for safety-critical status flags.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#090D18`):** Deep abyssal midnight providing maximum baseline contrast for luminous vector paths and glass surfaces.
- **Secondary Canvas / Recessed Voids (`#0F172A`):** Structured dark navy used for structural navigation panels, sub-drawers, and inset telemetry backdrops.
- **Elevated Glass Layers (`#151D2E`):** The primary structural card and container tone, overlaid with semi-transparent alphas to support blur filtration.
- **Structural Boundary Outline (`#22304A`):** 1px delineations for container segregation and radar gridlines.
- **Primary Brand Accent (`#7657F6`):** Electric Purple reserved for core interactive triggers, primary state anchors, and AI processing nodes.
- **Secondary Brand Accent (`#8B5CF6`):** Neon Violet used for secondary interactive paths, user avatar halos, and citizen-facing status glows.
- **Highlight / Synthetic Intelligence Accent (`#06B6D4`):** Pure Cyan indicating automated routing, active geo-nodes, confidence meters, and live map telemetry.
- **System States:**
  - **Success / Resolved (`#10B981`):** Emerald green for SLA targets met and field-verified resolutions.
  - **Warning / Nearing Breach (`#F59E0B`):** Amber for approaching escalation horizons and moderate triage demands.
  - **Critical / Emergency (`#EF4444`):** High-saturation crimson red for hazards, infrastructural failures, and immediate citizen safety escalations.
- **Text & Data Contrast:**
  - **Primary (`#F3F4F6`):** Highest brightness value for critical metrics, headings, and primary readouts.
  - **Secondary (`#9CA3AF`):** Muted gray for supporting telemetry, tabular labels, and form values.
  - **Subdued / Muted (`#64748B`):** Slate for metadata timestamps, deactivated indicators, and grid lines.

### Role-Specific Adaptations
- **Citizen Experience:** Elevated use of `#8B5CF6` and soft background ambient diffusions; lower visual density; increased line spacing.
- **Manager Command Center:** Data-dense matrices illuminated with `#06B6D4` vector markers and `#7657F6` micro-indicators over `#151D2E` panels.
- **Field Worker Interface:** High-contrast tokens with elevated baseline luminance for outdoor sun legibility; functional semantic badges dominate the view.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is split into two specialized typefaces: **Geist** for natural structural reading and structural headlines, and **JetBrains Mono** for all operational parameters, machine-generated outputs, tracking codes, metrics, and timestamps.

### Usage Standards
- **Geist (Headlines & Body):** Employs tight negative tracking at display scales for a machined, compact, technical profile. Body text relies on natural kerning and strict height-to-body ratios to ensure dense reports remain legible.
- **JetBrains Mono (Data, Labels & Metrics):** Applied to telemetry readouts, ticket IDs (e.g., `TK-8942-CIV`), SLA countdowns, coordinates, and system logs. Monospacing ensures fluctuating numbers do not cause layout jank during live data streams.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by a responsive **12-column variable-density fluid grid** built on a strict 4px/8px incremental base rhythm.

### Grid & Density Controls
- **Desktop (Manager Command Center / 1440px+):** 12-column dynamic layout using `gutter-desktop` (24px) and outer margin `margin-desktop` (40px). Side rails for real-time ticket ingestion and inspector panels stay fixed at 360px and 420px, with the central GIS map and data canvas remaining fully fluid.
- **Tablet (Inspection / Operations Lead / 768px – 1439px):** 8-column layout. Drawers transform into slide-over panels. Canvas margin drops to `margin-tablet` (32px), gutter settles at `gutter` (16px).
- **Mobile (Field Worker & Citizen Portals / 320px – 767px):** 4-column layout with outer boundaries pegged to `margin` (16px). Cards span 4 full columns to maximize thumb reach and tap targets (minimum interactive dimension: 44px).

### Spacing Application
- `space-xs` (4px): Micro-gaps between status dot indicators and label texts; inline tag grouping.
- `space-sm` (8px): Inner padding for tight chip badges, input icon-to-text gaps, sub-metric separation.
- `space-md` (16px): Structural internal padding for standard data cards, list items, and form elements.
- `space-lg` (24px): Padding within primary control panels, separation between chart clusters.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section margins and master dashboard cluster separators.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this design system is created via layered dark luminance and back-surface blurring rather than muddy drop shadows. Surfaces mimic thick, low-iron optical cockpit glass layered over deep operational voids.

### Glass Stack Architecture
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Unadorned `#090D18` background. Grid overlays rendered in `rgba(34, 48, 74, 0.4)` 1px vector lines.
- **Level 1 (Docked Containers & Sidebars):** `#0F172A` at 90% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Outlines are 1px solid `rgba(34, 48, 74, 0.6)`.
- **Level 2 (Cards, Widgets & Interactive Panels):** Surface `#151D2E` with 75% opacity and `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`. Border is 1px solid `#22304A`. Top border incorporates a directional 1px highlight: `linear-gradient(90deg, rgba(118, 87, 246, 0.3) 0%, rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.1) 50%, rgba(34, 48, 74, 0.2) 100%)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Flyout Inspectors):** Floating `#151D2E` at 95% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`. Outer shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`, accompanied by a perimeter border of `rgba(118, 87, 246, 0.4)`.

### Luminous Depth & Glow Fields
- **Status Aura:** Critical cards carry an internal edge glow: `box-shadow: inset 0 0 16px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.15)`.
- **Active Node Accent:** Selected map telemetry points or card headers cast an ambient field: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

To mirror the precision of high-reliability avionics and technical operations terminals, this design system adheres to an angular, restrained radius hierarchy (`roundedness: 1`).

### Shape Classifications
- **Base Surfaces & Input Fields (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Applied to form text inputs, small badges, tabular selection rows, and contextual action buttons. Retains sharp edge clarity without raw box brutality.
- **Cards, Panels & Data Containers (`rounded-lg` / 8px):** The default container radius. Balances sleek digital modernity with architectural firmness.
- **Modals, Floating Toolbars & Major Canvases (`rounded-xl` / 12px):** Applied exclusively to floating tactical HUD overlays and large comparison viewpoints.
- **Interactive Micro-Elements (Capsules / Circular):** Status dots, circular radar sweeps, and toggle switches use full pill geometry (`9999px`) to immediately separate operational status tags from layout containers.

## Components

### Buttons & Tactical Triggers
- **Primary Action (Electric Purple):** Solid `#7657F6` with primary text `#F3F4F6`. Hover state shifts to `#8B5CF6` with an exterior blur glow: `0 0 16px rgba(118, 87, 246, 0.45)`. Active scale: `0.98`.
- **Secondary Action (Glass Ghost):** Background `rgba(21, 29, 46, 0.6)`, 1px border `#22304A`, text `#F3F4F6`. Hover triggers border transition to `#06B6D4` with text transition to `#06B6D4`.
- **Critical Remediation Button:** Background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.15)`, border `1px solid rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.6)`, text `#EF4444`. Hover state elevates fill opacity to 100% with white text.

### Chips, Badges & Status Indicators
- **Architecture:** 24px height, padding 0 8px, font: `label-sm` (`JetBrains Mono`, uppercase).
- **Dot Indicators:** 6px diameter circular node positioned left of text. Pulse animations (`scale(1.2)` infinite cycle) signify unresolved live telemetry.
- **Variant Configurations:**
  - *Active / Cyan:* Border `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.4)`, background `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.1)`, dot `#06B6D4`.
  - *Emergency / Red:* Border `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.4)`, background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.1)`, dot `#EF4444`.
  - *Warning / Amber:* Border `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.4)`, background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.1)`, dot `#F59E0B`.
  - *Resolved / Green:* Border `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.4)`, background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`, dot `#10B981`.

### Data Cards & Glass Panels
- Constructed using background `#151D2E` at 80% opacity, bordered by `1px solid #22304A`, rounded to 8px.
- **Header Structure:** 48px height with a continuous bottom separation line (`1px solid #22304A`). Left-aligned metric or title paired with a right-aligned live mono timestamp.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Base background `#0F172A`, 1px border `#22304A`, inner height 40px, text `#F3F4F6`, placeholder `#64748B`.
- **Focus Paradigm:** Border sharply transits to `#7657F6` with zero outer ring spread, replaced by a subtle box shadow: `0 0 10px rgba(118, 87, 246, 0.3)`.

### Specialized Platform Modules
- **Radar Map Cards:** Interactive cartography modules framed in Level 2 glass. Overlaid with coordinate lines, GPS sweep reticles in `#06B6D4`, and incident cluster dots color-coded by severity.
- **SLA Countdown Timers:** High-contrast `metric-display` components using `JetBrains Mono`. Background incorporates a horizontal progression hairline that shifts dynamically from Cyan to Amber to Red as breach thresholds approach.
- **AI Agent Collaboration Cards:** Distinctive cards with an animated, left-facing vertical edge line (`2px solid #7657F6`). Includes auto-summarized incident triage reports and confidence score gauges (e.g., `CONFIDENCE: 98.4%`).
- **Before/After Comparison Widget:** Interactive horizontal split-handle viewport embedded inside an 8px glass frame. The slider separator is a 2px vertical neon bar (`#06B6D4`) with a circular, non-obtrusive dual-arrow pill grip.
